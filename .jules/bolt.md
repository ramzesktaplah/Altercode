## 2026-03-30 - Pre-computing Language Tokens in Compose Highlighting
**Learning:** `SyntaxHighlighter.highlight()` is called frequently during Compose re-renders and live text field edits. Allocating `commonKeywords + setOf(...)` and token lists on every `highlight()` call causes unnecessary GC pressure. Pre-computing maps of keyword sets and line comment lists per `CodeLanguage` during `object` initialization, coupled with indexed loops for comment matching and `Char` overloads for single character appends, eliminates allocations in the hot path.
**Action:** When working on live syntax highlighting or text transformation utilities in Jetpack Compose, pre-compute language token maps at object/singleton initialization and use primitive/Char overloads to avoid per-keystroke allocations.
